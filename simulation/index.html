Bézier Trajectory Notes

Foot paths use cubic Bézier arcs. Control points P1/P2 are offset to create a natural parabolic arc with rapid heel-plant. This eliminates the foot-scrape artifact of linear interpolation while keeping servo motion smooth and bounded.

MAGI Leg Inverse Kinematics (IK) Algorithm & Debugger

1. Leg Architecture (3-DOF)

Each leg has three revolute joints:

  • Coxa (θc): Hip horizontal rotation
  • Femur (θf): Hip vertical swing
  • Tibia (θt): Knee swing

Link Specs:

Coxa (dc): 30 mm

Femur (l1): 90 mm

Tibia (l2): 90 mm

2. Analytical IK Solver

Coxa Angle (θc)

θc = atan2(y, x)

Effective Reach (Ld)

r = √(x² + y²)

Ld = √((r − dc)² + z²)

Tibia Angle (θt) — Law of Cosines

θt = π − arccos[(l₁² + l₂² − Ld²) / (2·l₁·l₂)]

Femur Angle (θf)

α = atan2(z, r − dc)

β = arccos[(l₁² + Ld² − l₂²) / (2·l₁·Ld)]

θf = α + β
